Privacy policy — db-v2-backup

Last updated: 2026-10-12

  • db-v2-backup is used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.
  • It accesses Google Drive with the drive.file scope only: it can access only the files it created itself.
  • The files it uploads are the owner's own backups, encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it sends no data to any party other than Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

Contact: the owner of the GitHub account tmk-commits.
